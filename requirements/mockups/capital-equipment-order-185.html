Standard Terms Shown to Buyer

  • A repair bill averages 35% of the item's list value (issue #188); each spares kit reduces a repair by 50%.
  • Failure probability rises each quarter on the issue #188 Weibull schedule, and is multiplied by natural disasters (earthquake, flood) and sabotage during strikes.
  • Spares cost increases by 50% if manufactured at the time of failure.
  • Standard repairs complete the following season.
  • Expedited repairs are ready in the same season only when spares and air freight are available.
  • Air freight requires 2 manufacturer freight credits, a Transport cargo aircraft, and available manufacturer spares.
