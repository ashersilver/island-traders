# Brief — Economy P3: consumer demand for end products (#84) (2026-06-11)

**Suggested owner:** Codex (economy rules + AI; needs its own calibration pass).
**Base off:** current `origin/pre-release`.
**Issue:** #84 (P3 in `requirements/economics-review-2026-06-10.md`).
**Depends on / interacts with:** P1/P2 (#82/#83, merged), the money-supply +
liveness instrumentation (#73), and the AI revenue advisory (#98).

## Why this is the lever

The mechanism layer (P1/P2/#98) is in, but calibration is unchanged: money
supply contracts ~52.8%/game and Farmer/Manufacturer sit at ~4–6% win rate. The
B1/B2 data shows end products are produced but **never consumed or traded**
(Finance, HealthServices, PassengerSeats, Courses, Vaccine all 0 consumed / 0
traded over 1000 games). There is **no final demand** for end products (review
**D3**). P3 creates it — and, done right, also eases the money-supply sink.

## The money-flow problem to solve first

The review frames the loop as "population **earns** (P2) → **spends** (P3)". But
P2 payroll (`game.py` `_process_payroll`) currently **destroys** the wage cash
(it's a pure sink — there is no household cash account). So "spending" has
nothing to spend. **P3 must close this loop**, or per-capita consumption funded
from island treasuries is just redistribution and won't touch the −52.8%
contraction.

**Required design:** introduce a per-island **household/consumer cash pool**
(`Player.household_cash`, additive, counted in the money-supply metric alongside
`dollops`/`personal_cash`):
1. **P2 payroll is redirected, not destroyed:** the per-season wage moves
   `island.dollops → island.household_cash` (workers get paid) instead of
   vanishing. (Amend `_process_payroll`.)
2. **P3 consumption is funded from `household_cash`:** households buy end products
   on the market, paying the producing island. Cash flows
   household_cash → producer.dollops — a real pull market that also recirculates
   the wage money instead of deleting it.

Net effect: payroll becomes a transfer (employer treasury → households →
end-product producers), not a faucet/sink imbalance. Watch the money-supply chart
— the goal is the contraction easing materially.

## Consumption model (mirror the sustenance pattern)

Today `TurnManager._consume_and_post_sustenance` (`engine/turn.py:410`) consumes
the population's Food meals and `market.post_demand(...)` for shortfalls. Add a
sibling `_consume_and_post_consumer_demand` per season:

- **Goods** — per-capita recurring consumption rising with island wealth (an
  Engel-curve step: wealthier islands consume more Goods/head). Quantity ∝
  `population` × a wealth-scaled factor. Fund from `household_cash`; buy from the
  Manufacturer (market or order book). New constant block (e.g.
  `GOODS_PER_CAPITA_BASE`, a wealth multiplier).
- **HealthServices** — demand generated by casualties/illness. Hook the existing
  casualty model (`CASUALTY_RATES`, `constants.py:731`) and the Doctor's seasonal
  peaks (Summer injuries / Winter illness): injured/ill workers consume
  HealthServices. Fund from `household_cash`/treasury; buy from the Doctor.
- **Vaccine** — uptake that prevents flu losses (ties to #49): islands buy Vaccine
  pre-emptively; vaccinated population suffers reduced illness/casualty next
  outbreak. Buy from the Doctor.

Post **funded** demand (the buyer actually pays and receives), not just a demand
signal — that's what makes it a real market for Manufacturer/Doctor.

## Scope

**In:** `household_cash` pool + payroll redirect; per-season consumer demand for
Goods/HealthServices/Vaccine; constants; money-supply metric includes
`household_cash`; AI sellers already respond to demand (and #98 advisory now sees
this structural demand) — verify they fill it.

**Out:** no new resources; don't rebalance base prices blindly (calibrate after);
keep the equity/recap work (#107) and freight friction (P4/#85) separate.

## Acceptance
- Tests: payroll moves cash to `household_cash` (not destroyed); a populated
  island consumes Goods/HealthServices each season and the producer's `dollops`
  rises; money-supply metric accounts for `household_cash`.
- `--games 1000 --seed 42`: **end-product traded volume rises off zero**
  (Goods/HealthServices/Vaccine), the **money-supply contraction eases**
  vs −52.8%, and **Manufacturer/Doctor (and Farmer via the wage→food loop) win
  rates move toward 1/7**. Report before/after win% + money-supply + the B1/B2
  trade volumes.
- APP_VERSION bump + RELEASE_NOTES (player-facing: end products now have real
  consumer demand; wages now circulate).
